01About us

Wisdom Students Scholarship Programme

An initiative of the Wisdom Islamic Students Organization, State Committee. The programme enables talented Muslim students to pursue higher education with confidence through targeted financial support and personal guidance.

Financial assistance

Support for tuition fees, hostel expenses and essential study materials.

Training camps

Workshops and seminars that strengthen academic, personal and professional skills.

Awareness programmes

Guidance on government and private scholarships to make the most of the support available.

Transparent process

Selection based on document verification and a personal interview.

A limited number of scholarships are awarded each year as decided by the committee.

03How it works

From application to scholar, all in one portal

Your application, documents, interview and renewals live in one digital file. You always see what is done and what is needed next.

  1. Apply online

    Create an account with Google or your email, fill in the form and save as you go.

  2. Upload documents

    Add each required document from your phone. The checklist shows what is accepted or needs re-uploading.

  3. Verification

    The committee reviews your records, and a local Wisdom volunteer may visit to verify details.

  4. Interview

    A personal discussion to understand merit and financial need. You will receive the date and venue in the portal and by email.

  5. Decision and payment

    Awards are decided by the committee. Selected students add their bank details securely in the portal.

  6. Six-monthly renewal

    Upload your latest result and fee receipt each half-year to continue until your course ends.

Keep these ready

  • Educational qualification certificates
  • Fee payment receipts
  • Recommendation letter
  • Ration card

Your personal checklist appears in the portal. Photos taken on a phone are fine.

04The Intellectual Meet

Scholars together, every six months

Twice a year our scholars gather for the State Intellectual Meet. Alongside the renewal interviews, the day brings expert sessions that help students plan their money, their studies and their next step.

Sessions are led by experienced speakers and cover managing money wisely, finding public and private scholarships, choosing the right path in higher education, and the latest developments in the sector.

06FAQ

Questions students ask

Still have a question? Contact us

Who can apply?
Deserving Muslim students from economically challenged families who are studying PG, degree, diploma, teacher training or research programmes.
Is the interview compulsory?
Yes. All applicants must attend the interview. If you cannot be there in person, you can request an online interview in the portal.
How do I know where my application stands?
Sign in to the portal. A tracker shows your stage and a To do list shows anything you still need to do. We also send updates by email.
I do not have a Google account. Can I still apply?
Yes. Create an account with any email address and a password. You will confirm your email with a code, and you can reset a forgotten password the same way.
Do I need to renew?
Yes. Every six months scholars upload their latest exam result and fee receipt and may be called for a short renewal interview.
Is my information safe?
Only committee members who need your details can see them. Documents are stored privately and never shared publicly. Read our privacy notice.
